Privacy Policy for library-vault

Last updated: 2026-11-15

library-vault is a personal application used by a single user (its owner) to copy his own files to his own Google Drive account.

Data accessed

The application accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, solely to upload and verify backup copies of the owner's photo library.

Data use and sharing

Files are transferred only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Data retention

Backup copies remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The application keeps no separate copy of file contents.
